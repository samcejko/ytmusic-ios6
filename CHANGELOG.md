# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-16)

The first version.

- YouTube Music without an account: Home, Explore (moods, genres, new releases, charts), search with suggestions and
  filters, album, artist and playlist pages, endless song radio, gapless playback straight from YouTube (AAC).
- Synced lyrics (LRCLIB, else YouTube Music's text; tap a line to jump there).
- Downloads: songs, albums and playlists play without the internet (Wi-Fi only if you want).
- Your own playlists, and playlists imported by their link from Apple Music, Spotify or YouTube.
- Apple Music: connect your library and its playlists, songs, albums and artists play from YouTube Music (every song
  found by its ISRC); add songs to your Apple Music playlists; likes go to Apple Music's "love" both ways; Apple Music
  charts in Explore without any account.
- Same loudness for every song, crossfade, a sleep timer, a data saver (48 kbit/s HE-AAC on mobile data), an editable
  queue kept between launches.
- iPad: a sidebar and, in landscape, "Up next" or the lyrics in a side panel. iPhone: tabs and a compact player bar.
- A second build for 64-bit devices with iOS 15 and newer (rootless jailbreaks, TrollStore).
